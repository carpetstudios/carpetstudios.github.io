Questions

How is my progress saved?

Junk Flight saves your progress on your iPhone only. There's no account, and nothing is stored on our servers. The game plays offline.

How do I report a bug?

Email us with what happened, what you were doing just before, and your iPhone model and iOS version if you know them. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot. If you're in the beta, you can also send feedback through the TestFlight app.

How do I join the beta?

Open the TestFlight beta link on your iPhone. If you don't have Apple's TestFlight app yet, it will ask you to install it first. Then accept the invite and install Junk Flight. The game isn't on the App Store yet; that's coming soon.
