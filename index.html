Junk Flight is a 3D launch-and-upgrade game for iPhone. Fly your junk as far as it will go, earn coins, bolt on better parts, and launch again, with a raccoon at the controls and plenty of slapstick crashes along the way.

  • Three worlds: Scrapyard, Sunset Coast, Rooftop Run
  • Upgrades you can see
  • Plays offline
  • No account needed
