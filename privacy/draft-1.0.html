Junk Flight and this website

Privacy Policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

The short version: we don't collect your data. Junk Flight shows ads from Google only when you choose to watch one, and Google collects some data to show them. You choose whether those ads can be personalized.

Junk Flight

  • There are no Carpet Studios accounts. You don't sign up or log in.
  • There's no analytics of our own in the game.
  • Your progress is stored only on your device. It isn't sent to us.

Ads

Junk Flight offers optional video ads from Google AdMob, for example to earn extra coins after a flight. An ad plays only when you tap to watch one.

When an ad loads, Google's ad software collects:

  • your IP address, which it uses to estimate your general location;
  • device identifiers: Apple's advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking, and otherwise identifiers limited to this app;
  • the ads you've seen and how you interact with the app and its ads, such as taps and video views;
  • app performance and crash information.

Google uses this to show and measure ads, to prevent fraud and to improve its services. We don't receive it; AdMob gives us only totals, such as how many ads were watched. Google's use is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Personalized ads and tracking

Junk Flight asks once, when you finish the opening walkthrough: Apple's prompt asks whether the game may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. It doesn't ask again.

  • Allow: Google may use Apple's advertising identifier to show ads based on your activity in other apps and websites.
  • Ask App Not to Track: you still get ads, but Google doesn't receive the advertising identifier.
  • To change your answer at any time, open your iPhone's Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking, and switch Junk Flight on or off.

Ad content

Ads in Junk Flight are limited to G-rated, family-safe content for everyone, and we don't ask your age. Junk Flight isn't a children's app, and an ad never plays unless you choose to watch one.

Consent in Europe, the UK and Switzerland

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Junk Flight shows Google's consent form before the first ad, where you choose how Google may use your data. To change your choices later, open Junk Flight's Settings and tap Privacy Choices.

Game Center

Junk Flight uses Apple's Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. Signing in is optional, and the game works without it. If you're signed in, your best distances and achievements are sent to Apple under your Game Center account, and Apple shares your Game Center name with the game to show the leaderboards. Other players may see your name and scores, depending on your Game Center settings. Apple holds this data under Apple's Privacy Policy; we don't store it.

Purchases

Junk Flight has no in-app purchases yet. When they arrive in an update, Apple will handle every payment. We'll learn only which item was bought, never your name or payment details.

Children

Junk Flight is made for a general audience and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you're a parent and believe your child has sent us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

This website

  • This site uses no cookies, analytics, or trackers.
  • It loads nothing from other companies: no outside fonts, scripts, or embeds.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep basic server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy statement. We don't have access to those logs.

Sharing

We don't receive personal data, so we have none to sell or share. Google and Apple collect the data described above directly, under their own policies.

Beta testing and email

If you join the beta, Apple runs TestFlight under its own terms. Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you send through TestFlight with us. If you email us, we'll use your message only to reply to you.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
