Junk Flight and this website

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

The short version: we don't collect your data.

Junk Flight

  • Junk Flight doesn't collect any personal data.
  • There are no accounts. You don't sign up or log in.
  • There's no analytics or tracking in the game.
  • There are no ads and no in-app purchases at this time.
  • Your progress is stored only on your device. It isn't sent to us or anyone else.

This website

  • This site uses no cookies, analytics, or trackers.
  • It loads nothing from other companies: no outside fonts, scripts, or embeds.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep basic server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy statement. We don't have access to those logs.

Sharing

We don't sell or share any data, because we don't collect any.

Beta testing and email

If you join the beta, Apple runs TestFlight under its own terms. Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you send through TestFlight with us. If you email us, we'll use your message only to reply to you.

Changes

If this changes, for example if we add ads to Junk Flight, we'll update this policy and the date above.
